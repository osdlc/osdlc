# Reading list

A small example corpus: a shelf of books, the people who wrote them, and notes taken while
reading. It exists to show a corpus site.

- [Books](books/books.schema.md): one record per book, such as
  [Middlemarch](books/Middlemarch.md).
- [Authors](authors/authors.schema.md): one record per writer.
- [Notes](notes/notes.schema.md): reading notes. Each one names the books it is about.
