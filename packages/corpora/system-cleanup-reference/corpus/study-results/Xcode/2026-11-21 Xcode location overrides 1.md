Manage the locations of project-generated files in Xcode (https://help.apple.com/xcode/mac/current/en.lproj/deva52afe8a4.html) says, under Manage the locations of project-generated files:

> Use Locations preferences to specify different locations for project-generated files, to choose a version of the Xcode app for command-line tools such as xcodebuild to use, and to configure custom paths for your projects.
