dotnet nuget locals command (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/core/tools/dotnet-nuget-locals) says, under Options: -c|--clear:

> The clear option executes a clear operation on the specified cache type. The contents of the cache directories are deleted recursively.
