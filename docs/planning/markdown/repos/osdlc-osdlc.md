The home of the markdown ecosystem (decision D19): the Markdown libraries, the corpus-schema spec,
validator and suite, this plan and its tools. It also holds the sdlc CLI and the general libraries.
The profiles and the starter corpus come here too. Their place is not decided; the proposal is
`packages/profiles/*` and `packages/corpora/*`.
