An experiment: convert the fitness vault in sksizer/dev (`packages/vaults/fitness/vault/`) to a
corpus-schema corpus and open it as a pull request here, under `packages/corpora/`.

The vault is the case this trial is for: a useful data model backed by the research behind it. It
holds about 5,000 records in 18 folders. The model is exercises, muscles, muscle groups, movement
families, equipment, foods and nutrients. The research is about 2,700 evidence records and 700
sources: each evidence record names one field of one model record, the claim, and the source that
supports it. Every relationship is a wikilink.

What the trial should answer:

- Can the research half be a claims corpus (statements, study results and documents from
  `@osdlc/corpus-profile-claims`) that the model corpus names in `references`? Or is one corpus with
  both halves the better shape?
- How does a model record point at the evidence for one of its fields? Today the evidence record
  points at the model record and names the field. Say whether `x-ref` covers that or the spec needs
  something new.
- What does the vault have that corpus-schema cannot yet check? List each gap.
- The vault is generated by `build.mjs` from `research/`. Say whether the generator should write
  the corpus directly, or a converter should run after it.

Done when the corpus validates with `--strict`, the pull request is open, and the answers are
written in this record. A trial that ends with a list of gaps and no merged corpus is still done.

Before the pull request opens, the owner confirms the content may be public. sksizer/dev is private.
The vault's own content is CC BY 4.0, and imported rows (free-exercise-db, wger, OpenPowerlifting,
FoodData Central) keep their upstream licences, listed in the vault's `Attributions.md`.
