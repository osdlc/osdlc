# Vault

Status: current.

A folder of markdown notes and the files they link to, read as one linked whole. An Obsidian vault is one example.

The general term is moving to "corpus" (see [markdown-corpus](markdown-corpus.md)); "vault" stays in the older names until they are renamed.

## Not to be confused with

[markdown-corpus](markdown-corpus.md), the crate that models one.
